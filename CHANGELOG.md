# Changelog

## 2.1 (October 2026)

- Search and actions: press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac), or the search box in the menu, to jump to any student, class or page, or to import, report, play music and more.
- Zoho is now where you need it: on Today (with check-ins waiting), beside Add students, on each class's Students tab, and near the top of each student's profile.
- Music has its own place in the menu and on Today.

- Apps page: connect your own Zoho CRM, link students to Contacts and send attendance notes.
- Import students from Zoho Contacts into a class, filtered by tag, already linked.
- Update attendance fields on Contacts (percentage, status, last marked) so Zoho can report on them.
- Suggested check-ins: after three absences in a row or attendance below 90%, Here. proposes a Zoho Task. Nothing is created until you approve it.
- A built-in sample CRM in the sandbox and the demo, so all of this can be tried without connecting anything.
- Message a parent about an absence on WhatsApp or by email, from the student's profile.
- YouTube and Spotify player in a class's Tools.
- Choose the name the app shows for you.
- Sandbox: a separate practice space with its own tools.
- Content Security Policy and other security headers.
- New address: here.guilduk.com.

## 2.0 (October 2026)

- New name and brand: Here.
- Private accounts, with every query scoped to the signed-in user.
- Rebuilt around classes, with a Today page and one button per register.
- Paper register codes, absence reasons and notes.
- Paste a list of names to add a class.
- Month view, reports with a weekly chart, CSV export and print layout.
- Days off left out of percentages.
- Sample class for visitors.
- Help, privacy and terms pages. Download or delete all your data.
- Unit tests and GitHub Actions.

- University dissertation version: shared register with month grid and charts.
